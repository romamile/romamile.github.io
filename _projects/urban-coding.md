Urban Coding is a practice built around changing location, working inside unfamiliar surroundings and treating the noise of the place as material rather than distraction.

The aim is to make digital creative work more porous: architecture, people, movement and accidental constraints can all enter the process. PingOut emerged from one of these sessions.
