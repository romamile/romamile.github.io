Food&Code was designed as a social format rather than a conventional exhibition. People came to make, eat, show things, exchange skills and encounter digital art through the atmosphere of a shared meal.

Each edition could change shape while keeping the same hybrid logic: workshop and exhibition, technical experimentation and cooking, invited work and things made on the day. The event itself was treated as a medium for putting people and practices in contact.
