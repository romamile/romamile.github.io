The Scribbler turns drawing into a performative gesture. Visitors make small figures in the air, populate a digital environment with them, and then shift from creator to conductor as the creatures begin to move together.

A drawing is therefore not only an image but the beginning of a behaviour. The interaction moves from making individual forms to influencing a small collective.
