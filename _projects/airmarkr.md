AirMarkr explored spatial drawing before mobile 3D capture became commonplace. The project investigated how a handheld device could be used to leave marks directly in real three-dimensional space.

It grew into a start-up experiment around 3D capture, spatial interfaces and the different forms those traces could take, from on-screen visualisation to physical output.
