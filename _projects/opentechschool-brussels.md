OpenTechSchool Brussels combined event organisation, curriculum building and teaching. The emphasis was on making technical learning social, approachable and open to participants with very different backgrounds.

Rather than a conventional class, the format relied on volunteer coaches, reusable open teaching material and a community that could keep learning from itself.
