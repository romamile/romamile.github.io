Created rapidly for Food&Code #2 in Lyon, Sucker X Sucker embraces a deliberately light and improvised form. It sits somewhere between interactive gag, social experiment and event object.

Its interest is less in technical complexity than in how directly an object can address people and pull them into a shared joke.
