My research sits between **language evolution, artificial behaviour, swarm robotics and embodied interaction**. I am interested in questions that are difficult to separate cleanly into “linguistics” or “robotics”: how agents come to coordinate, how individual histories produce social structure, and how an action can acquire communicative meaning through use.

Robots and simulations are useful to me because they force the assumptions to be explicit. Perception, memory, learning and interaction can all be specified, changed and compared — while the resulting behaviour still has to emerge through repeated encounters between agents.
